
# E-Commerce Marketplace Structure
 
## What Lazada, Amazon, and Shopee have in common
 
All three are **e-commerce marketplaces**: online platforms that connect sellers with buyers, providing storefronts, payment processing, and logistics/delivery support. Amazon operates globally; Lazada and Shopee focus on Southeast Asia. All three share the same core structure below.
 
## Structure
 
```
                    E-Commerce Marketplace
                    (Amazon · Lazada · Shopee)
                              |
              ----------------------------------
              |                                |
      Third-Party Sellers              Platform as Retailer
   (independent merchant channel)      (platform buys & resells stock)
              |                                |
   Merchant lists & prices            Platform purchases
      own products                       inventory
              |                                |
   Platform hosts storefront          Platform sets price
      & payment                          & owns stock
              |                                |
   Seller fulfills, or uses           Platform ships as
   platform logistics (e.g. FBA)      retailer of record
```
 
## Details
 
### Third-Party Sellers
Independent businesses or individuals list and sell their own products through the platform. The platform provides the storefront, payment processing, and often fulfillment support, but doesn't own the inventory.
 
- **Amazon:** "Marketplace" sellers (labeled "Sold by [Seller Name]")
- **Lazada / Shopee:** most shops on the app operate this way
### Platform as Retailer
The marketplace company itself purchases inventory and sells it directly, acting as the retailer of record rather than just a middleman.
 
- **Amazon:** "Ships from and sold by Amazon.com"
- **Lazada:** LazMall and some direct retail operations
- **Shopee:** Shopee Mall (official brand/store partnerships; lighter on direct retail than Amazon)
## Key distinction
 
| | Third-Party Seller | Platform Itself |
|---|---|---|
| Owns inventory | No | Yes |
| Sets price | Seller | Platform |
| Retailer of record | Seller | Platform |
| Examples | Small shops, independent brands | Amazon Retail, LazMall, Shopee Mall |
